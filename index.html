Location history

User guide

Open an export, choose a view, then narrow the dates. All dates and hours use UTC.

Open your history

Choose Open location history or drop files onto the map. Select several files together to merge them; exact duplicates are removed. A new import replaces the current history.

Google Takeout, phone Timeline exports and timestamped GeoJSON points are supported, including JSON.GZ. Extract ZIP archives first. Use Load sample ↗ to try the public example.

Export Google Timeline as JSON

Export from the phone holding your history. Menu names may vary.

Android
System Settings → Location → Location services → Timeline → Export Timeline data → Continue. Choose a folder and save. Google’s instructions ↗
iPhone / iPad
Google Maps → Profile → Settings → Location & Privacy → Export Timeline data → Save to Files. Google’s instructions ↗
Legacy Takeout
For older cloud history, open Google Takeout ↗, deselect all, select Location History (Timeline) if available, and export JSON. Extract the ZIP and open Records.json, Location History.json or the monthly semantic-history files. Phone-stored Timeline uses the exports above.

Choose a visualization

Heatmap
Density of nearby locations. Adjust Spread, Intensity and Opacity.
Hexagons
Density by cell. Adjust Cell size; click a cell for Place history.
Points
Individual locations. Click for details, an accuracy circle, and ← → to browse points.
Journeys
Connected tracks. Choose Straight or Smooth and adjust line thickness. Long gaps and excluded hours break tracks.

Heatmap and Hexagons measure recorded locations, recorded days or estimated stationary time. Their colours show relative density in the current view.

Points and Journeys colour by Time, Speed or Altitude using the selected palette. Grey means unknown. Large views show representative points; zoom in for detail. Smooth curves only change the drawing.

Date window & filters

  • Day Week Month Quarter Year resize and lock the window length. Drag either thumb to move the fixed window. Click the active period again to unlock it without changing dates; the thumbs then resize independently. All time restores the full history and turns off when the range is narrowed.
  • ▶ Play ← Back → Forward step by the selected period. Click a histogram bar to select its dates.
  • Hours Accuracy Speed Altitude open range sliders and exact values. Reset clears a filter. Unknown measurements stay included unless you disable Include unknown.
  • Devices and Source appear when several choices exist.
Track quality

Outliers are checked across sources within each device, before display filters. Journeys connects the remaining points when the movement is plausible.

Off
Keep all locations.
Outliers
Remove speed spikes relative to nearby movement. A lower allowance is stricter.
GPS drift
Reduce stationary GPS noise and remove short outliers with the default allowance. Stationary speeds become 0; uncertain speeds become unknown. Context window sets how far to look for nearby recordings.

Place history

Click a hexagon or choose Draw place area, click corners, then Finish area. Undo corner removes a corner; Esc cancels. For keyboard drawing, pan the map, press Space to add its centre and Enter to finish.

Switch between Calendar, Stays and By day. The calendar has Year, Quarter, Month, Week and hourly Day views; tables sort by column. Choose Current window or All dates; the other filters still apply.

Track length counts only the parts inside the outline. Average and median speeds are weighted by time there, including known stops; unknown speeds are excluded.

Select a date to update Date window and reset Hours to the full day; select an hour for that hour only. This also works with a point’s date and time links. Stays separated by less than 15 minutes merge. Time and distance are estimates; sparse or inaccurate recordings limit precision. Altitude is the height recorded in the file.

Maps & privacy

Choose Roads, Terrain, Satellite, Hybrid or Dark, then a provider. Fit fits the filtered history; in Journeys it fits connected tracks. The compass resets rotation and tilt where supported.

Files stay in browser memory. Clear or reload removes them. Settings are saved; imported locations and the map camera are not. The selected map provider receives requests for the area you view.

Google Analytics and Yandex Metrica measure site usage. Yandex Session Replay hides the map and history controls. Map SDKs, jsDelivr and Google Fonts also make network requests. Imports are limited to five million locations and available device memory.
