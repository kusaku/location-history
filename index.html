Location history

User guide

Open one or more Google Takeout Records JSON files, monthly semantic history files, or an on-device Timeline JSON export. Gzip, our original delta-compressed sample, and timestamped GeoJSON points work too. A new import replaces the current history; files selected together are merged and exact duplicates removed.

Visualization modes

Heatmap blends nearby locations. Hexagons groups them into cells that adapt to zoom. Points shows up to 20,000 representative locations in view. Journeys connects consecutive observations, breaking at gaps over six hours, excluded records, dateline crossings, or implausible speeds.

Density metrics

Measure density by records, distinct UTC days per cell, or estimated stationary time. Time uses recorded visit duration or stationary intervals of at most 30 minutes within 200 metres, clipped to the observation’s UTC day and chosen hours. It is an estimate, not a complete measure of time spent. Colour strength adapts to the 95th percentile of occupied cells in view; compare patterns, not absolute values between zoom levels. Spread changes the radius in screen pixels. Click a hexagon or point for details.

Privacy & performance

Your imported history is kept in memory, never uploaded or saved. Clear or reload to remove it. The selected map provider receives normal map requests for the area you view, jsDelivr serves MapLibre, and Google Fonts serves the typefaces. No analytics or session recording runs here. Imports are limited to two million points and 512 MB per uncompressed file.

Base maps

Choose a base map in Map settings. OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap show streets; Esri provides satellite imagery. Google provides roads and terrain; Yandex provides streets. Availability and provider terms apply. Switching maps keeps your imported history and filters.
